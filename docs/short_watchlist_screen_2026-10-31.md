# Short-watchlist research: 2026-10-31 expansion

This note documents **18 candidate additions** to `config/short_watchlist.csv`, screened against the existing FinanceAlerts long universe and PaperTrader securities. All figures are historical and in the companies' reporting currencies unless otherwise specified. Source cutoff: 31 October 2026. A bearish watch is **not** a recommendation to buy a put today.

## Selection rule and configuration

- Prioritize structurally weak economics, refinancing risk, dilution, uncertain commercialization, or unusually large valuation expectations without demonstrated earnings. Exclude sound sector leaders merely trading at a premium.
- `active` means price monitoring and the existing RSI(14) >= 70 **Short Watch** trigger; it does **not** mean a put is currently attractive, liquid, or available at the user's broker.
- `research_only` means no price scans or alerts. Reasons include low-priced underlying, sparse or unknown put liquidity, incomplete accounting, or improving fundamentals that warrant a second review.
- Prices, borrow availability, expiration-specific spread/open interest, option IV versus realized volatility, and earnings dates must be rechecked before any trade. Extreme put IV and limited downside on sub-$2 shares can defeat a correct bearish thesis.
